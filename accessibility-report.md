# **Accessibility Report**

1. Equitable Use
   - MovieMatch has a local movie and TV database to allow users with low internet access to use our search algorithm without TMDb API access.
   - We offer an adult content search filter to provide a family friendly environment for all user age demographics.
   - In the future, we may implement speech-to-text capabilities in our search.

2. Flexibility in Use
   - MovieMatch has a settings page with options to change font size and display appearance with dark mode.
   - In our settings, we also allow users to turn off the adult content search filter.

3. Simple and Intuitive Use
   - Each label on our buttons leads users to the page with that respective title, aligning with user expectations.
   - When changing username or display name, our app informs users whether the switch was successful or not, providing feedback after task completion.

4. Perceptible Information
   - MovieMatch uses visual posters for each movie and TV show to accommodate users with varying levels of literacy.
   - In the future, we may implement zoom capabilities in our settings to add legibility.
  
5. Tolerance for Error
   - Incorrect passwords display a warning and only lock out after three incorrect attempts.
   - In the future, we may implement spell check for search queries to allow for more spelling error tolerance.
  
6. Low Physical Effort
   - MovieMatch offers dark mode to reduce eye strain.
   - In the future, we may minimize the number of pages users have to traverse through
   - (ie. `add to watchlist` -> `user's watchlist view` instead of `add to watchlist` -> `previous search` -> `home page` -> `user's account` -> `user's watchlist view`)

7. Size and Space for Approach and Use
   - Our buttons and fields are centred in the middle of the page to provide a clear view to both sitting and standing users.
   - In general, this principle is more applicable to physical environments than our desktop app.

MovieMatch would be marketed to movie and TV fans who want both a personal media tracker and a way to connect with others who share their interests. Its core features let users search for movies and shows, log what they have watched or want to watch, assign ratings, and write reviews. Users can also search for other accounts, view their public watchlist and watch history, send messages, and block unwanted contacts, creating a small social network centred on shared viewing habits.

The primary audience is people in their late teens through mid-thirties who already use watchlists or rate media and would enjoy comparing their tastes with friends rather than browsing streaming services alone. MovieMatch also provides personalized recommendations based on a user’s ratings, watch history, and watchlist, with a brief explanation for why each title was suggested. A secondary audience includes university students and campus film societies that want a shared, subscription-free space to track screenings, discuss films, and compare ratings.

Currently, MovieMatch does not support language translation, making it less likely to be used by non-English speaking demographics. Furthermore, it is only available as a desktop application, so mobile/tablet users will likely be dissuaded from using it as well.
